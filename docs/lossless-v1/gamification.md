# Gamification

This is a savings toy. The rules stay small so the player can make the story.

## Rules

- The deposit comes back, minus a Solana fee that is a fraction of a cent.
- One existing ticket wins. The random value does not draw lottery numbers for a match.
- Odds are the deposit share.
- Only harvested yield is the prize.

## Toys

- Pick a campaign: USDC now, SOL staking yield next, USDY later.
- Name the ticket.
- See the prize grow as other deposits arrive.
- Share the settlement transaction. The player is the storyteller.
- Points show time in the vault: one point per dollar per day. They burn on withdrawal. They can weight a vote. They do not change odds and they do not pay yield.

## Cut

- A loss if you do not win.
- A fee on the way out.
- A point that expires into nothing.
- An odds boost sold for the token.
- A number-match that often has no winner. That is a later rollover mode, not V1.

## Why this can grow

The pool grows only if the prize is visible before close and the winner is paid in the same transaction. A $10,000 pool at 4% for seven days makes about $8. A $1,000,000 pool makes about $770. The first will not travel. The second can, if the harvest and the payout are public. Do not invent a points token to fake the prize.
