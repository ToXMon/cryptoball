# Tokenomics

The token is not required to save. Revenue exists only after a harvest.

## Fee

Ten percent of the harvest is the protocol fee. Ninety percent is the prize. On a $1,000,000 pool at 4% for seven days, the prize is about $693 and the fee is about $77. No fee is taken from principal.

## Flywheel

The keeper uses the fee to buy the token and burn it, or sends it to the DAO treasury. Voters set that split. Publish the swap and the burn next to the settlement. A buyback does not set a price. It gives the fee a use. A larger pool makes a larger harvest. A larger harvest makes a larger burn.

## Utility

- Opening a campaign locks one token and sets the adapter and the cap.
- A vote is locked tokens plus points. A depositor with no token still has a vote. A token holder who never deposits has a smaller vote.
- Voters choose the next adapter, the fee split, and the campaign cap.
- Voters cannot spend principal.

## Not utility

- The token does not change odds.
- Points do not pay yield.
- Staking does not boost the chance to win other people's yield.

A token with a buyback can be treated as a security in some jurisdictions. This file is a product design, not a placement.
